## Predicted residue if achieved

Verification identifies and retains the exact executables whose behavior was
tested. The recorded hashes match those retained bytes when inspected later.
Another Cargo build can produce different executable hashes without destroying
the old artifact or confusing its historical evidence with current evidence.

Each verification attempt has an immutable bundle with source scope, binary
identities, commands, toolchain/configuration provenance, outcomes and useful
observations. Checks execute the chosen binaries. Mutation, incomplete publication
or an attempted overwrite is detected and cannot become passing evidence.

The original Watch Warp has truthful fresh acceptance evidence covering every
predecessor input path. Its accepted layers and historical observations survive.
Full-scope refresh works under an explicit bounded selection, while legacy default
limits and containment protections remain effective.

workflow, retain useful intelligence in Eventness and share optional trace-ID
signatures through Watch. Ordinary coding and unselected demo tests remain simple.

cause as far as it is established and retain unanswered questions as uncertainty.

Eventness. Its slices attempt its own predicted final product. Preserve the
failure as failure while carrying the understanding forward.
