KNOW YOUR TABLE

A good hand starts here.

Get closer to 21 than Ada without going over. Face cards count as 10; an ace is 1 or 11, whichever helps your hand.

Hit
Take another card. At 21, your hand automatically stands.
Stand
Keep your hand and let the dealer play.
Double down
Double your initial bet, take exactly one card, then stand. Available on your first two cards when you have enough chips.
Split pair
Split two cards of the same rank once, for a second equal wager. Play the highlighted hand, then the other. Double after split is allowed on non-aces. Split aces receive one card each and stand. Split 21 pays 1:1, not blackjack. No re-splitting.
Blackjack
An ace and a ten-value card on the initial deal pays 3:2. A normal win pays 1:1. A push returns your bet.

Ada checks for blackjack and stands on soft 17. Fresh deck each round. One split per round. No insurance or surrender. Bets use even amounts for exact payouts. Your table lasts until server restart or two hours without activity. All chips are simulated.
